Support

We usually reply within one business day.

Contact us

Email team@backit.live for anything — payment issues, passes that won't scan, account problems, or venue partnership enquiries. Include the phone number you signed up with so we can find your account quickly.

Common questions

My payment was charged but I didn't get a pass. If a payment is captured and a pass can't be issued, the charge is refunded automatically. If you don't see the refund within a few days, email us with the payment reference from your bank or UPI app.

My pass won't scan at the door. Make sure your screen brightness is up and the QR code fills the frame. If it still fails, door staff can look you up; email us afterwards and we'll make it right.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete Account in the app. This is immediate and permanent.

I run a venue and want to join SkipIt. Email us — we'll set up your listing and door-staff access.
