Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what data the SkipIt consumer app and the SkipIt Venues app collect, why, and what control you have over it.

What we collect

Phone number. Used to create and sign in to your account via one-time password. It is your account identifier.

Purchase and pass data. The venues, passes, and table reservations you buy, amounts paid, and pass validation events (when and where a pass was scanned). We need this to issue passes, admit you at the door, and handle payment disputes.

Payment data. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive payment status and reference identifiers only — we never see or store your card number, UPI PIN, or banking credentials.

Location (optional). With your permission, the consumer app uses your device location to show venues near you. Location is used on-device for sorting and is not stored on our servers. You can decline the permission and browse normally.

Camera (venue app only). The SkipIt Venues app uses the camera solely to scan guest QR codes at the door. Camera frames are processed on-device and never uploaded.

Push notifications (optional). If you enable notifications, we store a device token to send you order and pass updates. Declining does not affect the rest of the app.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Where data lives

Account, venue, and pass data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase. Payment processing runs on Razorpay. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

Account and purchase records are kept while your account is active and as long as we are required to keep transaction records under applicable law. Expired unpaid orders are purged automatically.

Your controls

You can delete your account at any time from the profile screen in the app. Deletion removes your account and personal data from our systems, except transaction records we must retain by law. You can also revoke location, camera, and notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

The Service is intended for adults of legal age for the venues concerned and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you in the app before they take effect.

Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: team@backit.live.
